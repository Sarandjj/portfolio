Seven homepage concepts

Each one is a working page with real content and one interactive idea. Open each, scroll, click things, try it on your phone. Then tell me the number you like, or a mix (for example "colours of 1, journey of 4"). Lesson lines in the journeys are sample text.

CONCEPT 1 MY PICK

Pearl City

A harbour scene of Thoothukudi that follows the time of day, Muthu the pearl in a shell, two ways in, and an app shelf.

Try: the time buttons and click the shell. Inspired by: shanesation, icon.museum.
Feels: warm, local, memorable.

Night Harbour

A dark, painterly moonlit sea, elegant serif type, the journey in chapters with lessons, and projects as engraved "FIG" drawings with one measured proof each.

Try: scroll to the FIG cards, hover to draw them. Inspired by: anzalabidi.dev.
Feels: senior, calm, thoughtful.

CONCEPT 3

Engineer's Notebook

Graph paper, technical frames with corner markers, camera-viewfinder project cards with a big proof number, filters, and a "find the bug" mini game.

Try: the filters and click the buggy line of code. Inspired by: harshitr.me, pixel-perfect.space.
Feels: technical, credible, developer-first.

Morning to Night

Try: scroll slowly through the ride. Inspired by: shanesation.tech.

CONCEPT 5

The Workshop Desk

Your desk as the home page: the laptop opens projects, the phone opens demos, the chai is About, the CV is the recruiter view. "Found 0 / 6" turns it into a small game.

Try: click everything that sparkles. Inspired by: v2.kidus-yohannes.engineer.

CONCEPT 6

Bold Blocks

Butter yellow, thick black outlines, chunky stickers with your numbers, a moving stack banner, and colourful app cards that tilt.

Try: hover and press the buttons. Inspired by: cuedesign, neo-brutalist sites.

CONCEPT 7

Kolam

A Tamil kolam that draws itself as the hero, "Vanakkam" in Tamil, a calm paper-and-indigo palette, and kolam-style marks for each app.

Try: reload to watch it draw, hover it to spin. Inspired by: Tamil kolam art.
Feels: elegant, cultural, grown-up.
